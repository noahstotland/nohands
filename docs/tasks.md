# Tasks

How a request the grammar does not understand runs. Back to the [README](../README.md). Keys are in [Configuration](configuration.md).

## Multi-step tasks

A request the grammar does not understand runs as a task: look at the frontmost app (title, URL, up to `maxObservedElements` numbered elements), ask the model for the next step, run it, wait for the screen to settle (`settleMs` at most), repeat. Each answer has an `intent`, a `status` (`continue`, `done`, `stuck`, `ignore` or `ask`) and actions. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-task-agent-design.md`.

- **Ending.** On `done`, `stuck`, the step cap (`maxTaskSteps`, `maxMediaTaskSteps` for play or watch), or the same actions three times running. A held mouse button is released unless `done`. `ignore` after earlier steps shows `stopped: <intent>`.
- **Media.** A play, watch or listen goal is done only when media plays: after `done` NoHands looks once more; a visible Play button gets one more model call (`not playing yet`), then `stuck`. `wait_for` `media_playing` polls every half second.
- **Close and quit guard (code).** command-W is blocked unless the request contains close (closes, closing, closed) or shut; command-Q unless quit (quits, quitting), exit (exiting) or shut. Whole words, per key; menu items follow by title ("Close Window" needs a close word, "Quit Safari" a quit word; "Exit Full Screen" is not held); a menu bar `click_in` follows the same title rule, and a Shut Down, Restart, Force Quit or Log Out item is refused whatever the request says. A blocked step runs nothing.
- **Confirmation guard.** A step pressing a button or menu item named send, delete, remove, discard, pay, buy, purchase, checkout, confirm, submit, publish, post, unsubscribe, transfer, place order or empty trash waits unless the request already says that verb. The row shows `step n · say yes to <verb>` for 30 s; "yes", "confirm" or "do it" runs it (with an attention word set, start with it, so a "yes" from a TV does not count); anything else, silence or "stop" ends the task (`stopped: <verb> not confirmed`). A verb after "not" or "don't" in the request does not allow itself, up to a comma only when nothing guarded was negated or a new instruction follows ("don't delete, send or archive anything" does not allow send; "don't delete it, just send it" does). The guard sees every route to the button: its name, its label number, a grid cell or badge number, a click at the cursor or at a point, a drag released on it, and Return or Space on a focused button. A yes covers one step (`say yes to send and delete`), and the screen is read again after it: if a label the step uses changed, nothing runs and the step fails `element changed, look again`. Just before a press, the live title of what it lands on is checked too: a guarded button the step did not name and you did not confirm fails the step with `<verb> not confirmed, look again`, and the next step asks. Fixed phrases you say are not asked about by this guard; the ones marked `Asks first` in [Voice commands](voice-commands.md) wait for their own yes. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-nh-61-confirm-irreversible-design.md` and, for the routes, the re-read and the press-time check, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-nh-154-irreversible-confirm-gaps-design.md`.
- **Small-model slip fixes (code).** A `type_text` that is a whole web address becomes `open_url`; a click on a menu-bar item fails unless the request mentions a menu or the item's words (off while number badges show; a `click_in` on the menu bar names no number, so it is always checked). Site routes (YouTube, Google, Wikipedia, Amazon, Reddit) are fixed hints written by NoHands. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-nh-90-small-model-slips-design.md`, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-nh-45-site-playbooks-design.md`.
- **Questions.** With several matches and no way to choose, the row shows `step n · ? <question>` (cut to 80 characters) and your next words are the answer; 30 s of silence ends it `stuck: no answer`. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-nh-47-task-asks-question-design.md`.
- **Reading the page.** Generic links ("Watch", "Play", "Read more") list the 40 characters before them (`3 [link] Watch near: "Raptors vs Celtics 7:30 PM"`). `read_page` returns the window's visible text under `Read:` in the next step only, up to 4,000 characters, untrusted; none: `no text on this page`. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-nh-50-read-page-text-design.md`.
- **Planned goals.** A goal of four or more words about playing media or naming a website first gets one model plan of two to eight fixed-set steps (the model never writes a web address), run in code under the same guards and "stop"; a missed check (after 1.5 s) hands over to the step loop. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-nh-91-model-written-plans-design.md`.
- **Vision fallback (off by default).** With `"visionFallback": true`, Claude and Screen Recording, a JPEG of the focused window (at most 1200 px long side) is attached when the last step failed on a missing or stale target (`no element named …`, `element changed, …`), the model used `look`, or it answered `stuck` with no picture (`screen capture failed` and `screen recording not granted` show on the row). A close box clicked by point is not blocked by code (only the prompt says not to close anything the request did not ask to close), so keep `visionFallback` off if you cannot accept that risk. A point click happens only on the step that has the picture, as its first action, once per step. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-nh-41-vision-fallback-design.md`.
- **Pop-ups and extra tabs.** `close_tab` and `close_window` act only on a tab or window the task opened (never the tab the task navigated to); one that was already open is closed only when the request says close; `select_tab` is never blocked. Up to 20 tab titles go to the intent backend. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-nh-42-close-popups-until-video-plays-design.md`.
- **Follow-ups.** For `followUpWindowMs` (30 s) after a task ends (`done`, `stuck` or `stopped`; not `ignored` or `failed`), a request that starts with `no`, `nope`, `not that`, `not this`, `the other`, `other one`, `the next`, `next one`, `the previous`, `instead`, `also`, `and then`, `and also`, `go back to`, `actually` or `wrong one` is a follow-up (a lone "no", "nope", "actually", "also" or "instead" is not): the model also sees the earlier goal, its last 8 steps, how it ended and the screen it ended on (as data), and the media budget and site routes use the earlier goal too. A follow-up is never planned. Grammar commands in between leave the memory alone; a task that is not a follow-up, switching mode, sleep, pause and a backend switch clear it. Memory goes back one task's steps; `0` turns it off. CLI tasks have none. A model step in a macro (`macros` in config) is an ordinary task for this memory. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-nh-48-follow-up-commands-design.md`.
- **Route memory.** When a task ends `done` after at least two clean steps, NoHands saves its goal and the steps that worked to `~/.config/nohands/routes.json` (the last 50 tasks; the same goal on the same site replaces the older one). A later goal that shares at least two words with a saved goal, and at least 60% of the longer of the two (filler such as `the` and `on` ignored), gets those steps as a `Last time this worked:` hint on every step, as data: the model follows it only where the screen agrees, and every step still goes through the decoder and the guards, so a saved confirm-first click still waits for a spoken yes. A route for another site is skipped unless the goal names that site or the page is not on a site. Steps are kept by name (`click "Raptors vs Lakers"`, `open_url espn.com/nba/scoreboard`), never by label number; typed and searched text, question answers and what was on screen are never kept (see [Privacy](privacy.md)). `stuck`, `stopped`, `ignored` and failed tasks save nothing, and neither does a follow-up, which also gets no hint (it already carries the earlier task). A task that asks you a question saves the question, not your answer. The file is read once at launch and matching happens in memory, so a task never waits on the disk, and a file that cannot be read or written never stops a task. Say `forget that route` or `forget all routes` to drop routes; `"routeMemory": false` turns it off, and the CLI never uses it. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-nh-46-route-memory-design.md`.
- **Opening files.** `open_file` opens a file or folder by the words of its name, a standard folder, or the newest file of a kind. The model never gives a path: NoHands asks Spotlight and applies the same guard as the voice commands (inside your home folder, no `~/Library` or dot folders, never a program). Several matches fail the step (`several files match: ask the user to say open file <words>`) rather than listing names to the model. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-nh-58-open-files-design.md`.
- **Stopping.** "stop" ("cancel", "never mind", "abort", "stop the task", "stop stop", "no, stop") aborts the request in flight; rows show `stopped` and `stopping the task`. CLI: Ctrl-C.
- **Talking while a task runs.** Other speech is held (`held · runs after this task (say stop to cancel)`); only the newest is kept (the replaced row shows `dropped`); it runs after the task unless over 60 s old or you said "stop".

## agent-desktop harness

With [agent-desktop](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-desktop) installed (`npm i -g agent-desktop` or `brew install agent-desktop`, macOS 13+), tasks observe and act through it instead of NoHands' own accessibility walk. The menu shows `Harness: agent-desktop <version>` or `Harness: native (…)` (`restart NoHands to use it` after an install or grant); `nohands-cli desktop-status` prints it. Details: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-agent-desktop-harness-design.md`.

- **Observation.** The model sees each field's value (`= "cats"`) and states (checked, selected, disabled, expanded, offscreen); an open sheet or menu is listed next (`Surface: sheet`).
- **Actions** land on the element (a real click only if agent-desktop delivered nothing): `set_value`, `type_into`, `clear`, `select`, `check`/`uncheck`/`toggle`, `expand`/`collapse`, `focus`, `scroll_to`, `scroll_element`, `drag`, `menu_item` (exact title; works without agent-desktop), `click_in` (`dock`, `menubar`, `sheet`, `alert`, `popover`, `menu`, `menu_bar_extras`), `close_app` (request must say quit, exit, shut or close), `window`, `clipboard_set`, `read_clipboard`, `read_value`, `read_notifications`, `dismiss_notifications` (results under `Read:` next step), `wait` with `wait_for`.
- **Fallback.** Chosen once per task; if agent-desktop cannot read the frontmost app (no window, another Space, minimized, locked screen) the task runs natively. Grammar commands and dictation never use it.

The harness keys are in [Configuration](configuration.md#agent-desktop-harness-keys).

**Claude vs. the local model.** Use Claude for tasks: with `qwen2.5:7b-instruct` the cold first request timed out, the warm one answered `ignore`, and no task has completed. A step costs 5 to 20 s on an M1 Pro (about 2,200 prompt tokens), hence `localLlmDeadlineMs` (30 s) and `localMaxObservedElements` (40). NoHands warms the model every 20 minutes while listening.
