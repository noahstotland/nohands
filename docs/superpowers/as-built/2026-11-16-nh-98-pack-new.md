## Differs from the spec

- An item that is Empty Trash shows as `already a NoHands phrase`, not `irreversible`: the grammar already parses "empty trash", and that check comes first. `Send`, `Delete…` and the like are `irreversible`.
- The irreversible check also runs on the `menu_item` form of an item that has a shortcut, so a shortcut cannot skip it.
- Curly apostrophes in titles become `'` in phrases; the character `" "` maps to the space key.
- With no `--app` and no app in front, the command stops with `no app in front to read; bring one forward and try again`.
- With `--out`, an existing file is caught before the 3 s wait; the file is created with `O_EXCL | O_NOFOLLOW`.
- A bad bundle ID or a draft with nothing in it is reported by `PackDraft.make` as a `problem` (`badBundleID`, `nothingToPut`, `invalid`).
- The reader reports when its time or item budget stopped it (`read(pid:)` returns `(entries, stoppedEarly)`), and `pack new` then prints a notice that later menus may be missing.
